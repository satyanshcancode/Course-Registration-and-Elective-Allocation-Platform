Course Registration and Elective Allocation Platform

When course registration opens, a large number of students try to register
within the same short window; a simple first-come-first-served approach
rewards fast internet connections over merit or need, and oversubscribed
electives end up resolved through informal appeals to the department.

Course catalogue showing live seat counts; eligibility pre-check before
the registration window opens; registration cart with a simulated atomic
submit; fair allocation logic for oversubscribed electives based on
preference and priority (mock scoring); waitlist with automatic promotion
as seats free up; add/drop screen; personal registration status and
history view.
